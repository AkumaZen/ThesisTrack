emitted `NULL` into a `GENERATED ALWAYS` column on INSERT (no `Computed()`

behavioral facts, not features that don't exist - closest fit in the

verifying it" is more precise than "invented." Component at fault: `M`
(specifically `knowledge/`, since both facts are reusable across every
remaining phase, not one-off environment trivia - see the promotion in Pass
4 below).

**Steps 9-11, structural pattern:** three router+service+schema groups

router registered in main.py, dependencies=[Depends(require_api_key)] at the
router level). This is the "multi-step pattern recurred 3+ times with a
stable shape" bar in refiner.md Pass 3, met within a single window.
Component: `K`.

No other signature: nothing was re-read 3+ times, nothing was re-derived
