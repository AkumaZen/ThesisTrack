# Thesis Tracker user guide

## Where things are

The top bar has five groups.
Pick a group, then a tab under it.

- Thesis: Companies, Review queue, Scorecard.
- Valuation: Watchlist, Master Tracker, Concalls, Red flags, Compare.
- Markets: Sector rotation, Breadth, Returns, Breakout scanner, Results.
- Filings: Announcements, Insider trades, Ex-dates.
- Alerts: Inbox, Tracked companies, Price alerts.

The account menu (your name, top right) holds Settings, Sector baskets (admin only), Team and access (admin only) and Change password.

Also good to know:

- Guidance has no tab. Press `u` to open it.
- Lists open from "Manage lists" on the Watchlist.
- Saved scans open from the "Saved scans" link on the Breakout scanner.
- Press `?` anywhere to see all keyboard shortcuts.

## Thesis

Write a thesis and review it.

1. Go to Thesis, Companies and press "+ New company".
2. Fill in the thesis, then press "Confirm" to save it.
3. Open the company and use the Quarterly Review panel, then "Submit Quarterly Review".
4. Check Review queue for items that need a look, and Scorecard for how your calls have done.

## Valuation

Watchlist: bear, base and bull valuations for the companies you follow.
1. Open Valuation, Watchlist and pick a list tab.
2. Use "+ Add company" to add one.
3. Open a company name for its full valuation page.
4. Use "Export" for Excel or Print.

Lists: keep several lists, each with colour flags.
1. On the Watchlist press "+ New list" and type a name.
2. Press "Manage lists" to open the Lists page.
3. Press "Flag" next to a company, pick a colour, add a short note and press "Save flag".

Master Tracker: what management promised against what was delivered.
1. Press "Add company", search for the company and press "Create tracker company".
2. Press "Load available quarters" on the company.
3. Tick the quarters to analyse.
4. Press "Analyse selected quarters".
Leave "Refresh research" unticked unless you want fresh documents.

Concalls: one row per tracked company and quarter.
1. Filter by quarter or sector, or search for a company.
2. Open "Data" and press "Refresh checks" to read the latest financials.
3. Press "Analyse N new transcripts" to read new calls.

Red flags: financial checks and how often guidance was kept.
1. Filter by sector or level (High, Watch, Clean).
2. Click "Red-flag score" or "Guidance kept" to sort.
3. Open "Data" to refresh the checks.

Compare: companies side by side.
1. Type a name or ticker under "Add a company" for each company, up to the limit shown.
2. Press "Compare".
3. Open "How this works" for how the metrics are chosen.

## Markets

Sector rotation: major sectors against Nifty 50.
1. Open "How this works" for the method.
2. Use the sort control to rank sectors.
3. Open "Data" to refresh or import.

Breadth: the share of stocks below their 50 and 200 day averages.
1. Read the chart, then the advances, declines and 52-week extremes below it.
2. Open "Data" to load history.

Breakout scanner: every stock, classified by stage.
1. Click a stage chip to narrow the list.
2. Pick a "Preset scan" from the dropdown.
3. Open "More filters" for extra signals, and use the sector and market cap dropdowns.
4. To keep a scan, open "Data", then "Save this scan", name it and press "Save".
5. Press "Saved scans" to reopen it later.

Results: the latest reported quarter for every company.
1. Search, or pick a quarter, industry or list.
2. Open "More filters" for sector baskets.
3. Open "Data" to load missing results.

## Filings

Announcements: every NSE announcement.
1. Pick a "Preset scan", or type a company or a "Keyword", then press "Apply".
2. Press "Save keyword" to keep a keyword.
3. Open "Your keywords" to see or remove them.

Insider trades: insider trades and substantial acquisitions.
1. Pick the disclosure type tab.
2. Filter by company, shareholder, category and buy or sell, then press "Apply".

Ex-dates: dividends, splits, bonus, rights and buybacks going ex soon.
1. Pick a type of action.
2. Read the list grouped by ex-date.

## Alerts

Inbox: price, sector and breakout alerts.
1. Use "Filter alerts by type" to narrow the list.
2. Open "Alert settings" to change thresholds or mute a type.

Tracked companies: announcements pushed to you.
1. Search for a company and add it.
2. Open "Notifications on this device" and allow notifications.
3. Press "Send test notification" to confirm it works.

Price alerts: be told when a price level is hit.
1. Search for a company.
2. Choose the condition in "When" and enter the level.
3. Press "Add alert".

## What changed in this update

- Pages are calmer: help sits under "How this works", extra filters under "More filters", and maintenance buttons under "Data".
