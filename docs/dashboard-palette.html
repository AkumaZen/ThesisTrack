How the two themes relate: only the ground and text tokens change between themes — --bg, --surface*, --border, --fg, --muted-fg. The four accent hues and the new --white token stay identical in both, which is what keeps the brand recognizable when switching. Buttons and pills that currently hardcode color:#050505 on an accent fill already work unchanged on the white ground.
